About APEX Engineering & Power Cooling Systems LLP

An industrial engineering partner for cooling tower performance, plant reliability, mechanical execution and industrial supply.

Who We Are

APEX Engineering & Power Cooling Systems LLP is an industrial engineering company providing Cooling Tower Services & Internal Equipment Supply, together with Mechanical, Fabrication & Engineering Services.

We support power, steel, cement, chemical, fertilizer, process and other industrial plants with inspection, maintenance, rehabilitation, shutdown and engineering requirements.

Our capability combines field execution with equipment supply, fabrication, erection and site engineering support, enabling coordinated execution for industrial maintenance and project requirements.
