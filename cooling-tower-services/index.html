COOLING TOWER ENGINEERING

Cooling Tower Inspection, Maintenance & Rehabilitation

Cooling tower performance directly affects plant cooling reliability and operating efficiency. APEX Engineering & Power Cooling Systems LLP supports cooling tower owners, EPC contractors and industrial plants with inspection, maintenance, refurbishment, rehabilitation and internal equipment replacement requirements.

Our scope covers natural draft and induced draft cooling tower applications, including IDCT and NDCT maintenance, refurbishment and shutdown-related works.

OUR SERVICES

Complete Cooling Tower Service Capability

Cooling Tower Inspection

Inspection and assessment of cooling tower condition, internal components, water distribution systems, structural elements and performance-related issues.

Cooling Tower Maintenance

Planned maintenance and corrective works for cooling tower equipment and associated mechanical systems.

Cooling Tower Refurbishment

Refurbishment and rehabilitation of cooling tower components to restore reliable operation and support improved performance.

IDCT & NDCT Services

Maintenance, refurbishment and related works for induced draft and natural draft cooling towers.

Internal Equipment Replacement

Replacement and supply support for fills, drift eliminators, nozzles, spray systems, headers, distribution piping, louvers and related components.

Shutdown & Annual Services

Cooling tower shutdown, annual maintenance and planned service requirements coordinated with plant maintenance schedules.
