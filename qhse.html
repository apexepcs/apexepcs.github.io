Quality, Health, Safety & Environment

A disciplined commitment to quality, people, safe execution and responsible environmental practices across our operations.

Our QHSE Commitment

APEX Engineering & Power Cooling Systems LLP is committed to maintaining strong standards of quality, health, safety and environmental protection across its engineering and industrial activities.

Our QHSE approach is intended to support responsible execution from initial inspection and planning through fabrication, equipment supply, installation, maintenance and shutdown execution.

We believe that quality workmanship, safe working practices, employee wellbeing and environmental responsibility are essential to dependable project execution.

Our Four QHSE Pillars

Q

Quality Assurance

Focus on quality-conscious engineering, workmanship, equipment supply and project execution.

H

Health & Wellbeing

Promote responsible working practices that support the health and wellbeing of personnel involved in our activities.

S

Site Safety

Safety-focused planning and execution for industrial, maintenance, fabrication and shutdown activities.

E

Environment

Maintain awareness of environmental responsibilities during industrial service and project execution activities.

Environmental Responsibility

Industrial service and maintenance activities can involve materials, water, waste, removed equipment and site operations. APEX Engineering & Power Cooling Systems LLP recognizes the importance of responsible environmental practices during such activities.

Our approach is to work in accordance with applicable site requirements and client environmental procedures while maintaining responsible housekeeping and material handling.

Areas of Attention

  • Responsible material handling.
  • Good housekeeping at work locations.
  • Controlled handling of removed materials and waste.
  • Avoid unnecessary contamination of work areas.
  • Follow client and site environmental requirements.
  • Promote efficient use of materials and resources.
