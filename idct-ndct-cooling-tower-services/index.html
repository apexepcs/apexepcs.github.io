COOLING TOWER ENGINEERING

IDCT & NDCT Inspection, Maintenance & Rehabilitation

Cooling tower condition directly affects plant cooling reliability, operating continuity and maintenance planning. APEX Engineering & Power Cooling Systems LLP supports industrial facilities with cooling tower inspection, maintenance, refurbishment and rehabilitation activities for IDCT and NDCT systems.

IDCT Cooling Tower Services

Services for induced draft cooling tower systems, including inspection, maintenance, refurbishment, internal replacement and shutdown-related execution.

  • IDCT inspection and condition assessment
  • IDCT maintenance and refurbishment
  • Internal equipment replacement
  • Fill and drift eliminator replacement
  • Nozzle and spray distribution works
  • Shutdown and annual maintenance support

NDCT Cooling Tower Services

Services supporting natural draft cooling tower maintenance, rehabilitation and internal equipment replacement requirements.

  • NDCT inspection and maintenance
  • Internal equipment replacement
  • Fill and drift eliminator works
  • Water distribution system works
  • Basin cleaning and desilting
  • Shutdown and rehabilitation support

Cooling Tower Internals Replacement

Replacement and supply support for major cooling tower internal components required during maintenance, refurbishment and shutdown projects.

  • PVC / PP / FRP fills
  • Drift eliminators
  • Cooling tower nozzles
  • Spray headers and distribution piping
  • Louvers and air-inlet components
  • FRP / HDPE / PVC components

Basin Cleaning & Related Works

Supporting works associated with cooling tower shutdowns, including basin cleaning, desilting and related internal maintenance requirements.

  • Hot water basin cleaning
  • Cold water basin cleaning
  • Desilting and debris removal
  • Sump and strainer related works
  • Basin liner related works
  • Shutdown execution support
